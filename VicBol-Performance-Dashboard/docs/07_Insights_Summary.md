# Insights Summary  
**Project:** VicBol Performance Analytics Case Study  

---

## 1. Overview

The dashboard shows a clear picture of how VicBol's marketing spend translates into traffic and revenue over the May–December 2025 period. Spend is by far the most volatile metric, moving between roughly 120M and 168M CZK from month to month. Revenue, in contrast, stays within a much narrower range. Because of this gap, profit remains negative throughout the entire period, and ROI never turns positive.

The data suggests that while higher spend does lead to more exit clicks and slightly higher revenue, the return doesn't scale at the same pace as the investment.

---

## 2. Seasonality

Seasonality is easy to spot:

- **June** shows a noticeable dip across spend, exit clicks, and revenue, aligning with typical summer slowdowns in ecommerce.  
- **November** is the strongest month in the dataset, driven by Black Friday and early holiday shopping.  
- **December** drops compared to November but still performs well relative to most other months, reflecting the usual tapering after peak shopping in late November.

---

## 3. Channel Performance

Channel performance is heavily concentrated:

- **Channel 22** accounts for the majority of both spend and revenue, making it the main driver of overall performance.  
- Even for Channel 22, the gap between spend and revenue is wide.  
- Several other channels show relatively high spend but contribute very little revenue, suggesting that budget allocation may not be fully optimized.

---

## 4. Traffic Efficiency

Traffic efficiency metrics reinforce the broader story:

- Exit clicks rise and fall almost in sync with spend, confirming that spend directly influences traffic volume.  
- CPC stays fairly stable, but given the high spend levels, even small inefficiencies add up quickly.  

Overall, the data points to a marketing setup where spend drives activity, but the return on that spend is limited. There are clear opportunities to refine channel strategy, adjust budget distribution, and better leverage seasonal peaks.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Rebalance spend across channels based on performance**  
   A large portion of the budget goes into channels that generate relatively low revenue. Reviewing channel-level efficiency and shifting spend toward higher-performing channels would help reduce wasted investment and improve overall ROI.

2. **Set clear efficiency benchmarks for each channel**  
   Introduce simple targets for CPC, ROI, or revenue contribution. Channels that consistently fall below these thresholds could be paused, tested with new strategies, or given reduced budgets.

3. **Align spend more closely with seasonal patterns**  
   The data shows predictable seasonal swings: a dip in June and a strong peak in November. Increasing spend ahead of high-demand periods and reducing it during weaker months could improve efficiency without sacrificing traffic.

4. **Investigate why revenue doesn't scale with spend**  
   Spend and exit clicks move together, but revenue grows much more slowly. Testing new creatives, audiences, or landing page improvements could help improve conversion rates and narrow the gap between spend and revenue.

5. **Deepen analysis at the channel and device level**  
   Performance varies across channels and devices. A more detailed breakdown could reveal where users are more engaged or more likely to convert, helping refine bidding strategies and improve overall efficiency.

---

## 6. Future Enhancements

1. **Add channel-level and device-level breakdowns**  
   Introduce channel-specific and device-specific views to uncover where performance is strongest and where spend may not be paying off.

2. **Build drill-through pages for deeper exploration**  
   Allow users to click into a channel or month and see detailed trends (spend, exit clicks, CPC, revenue). This turns the dashboard from a summary tool into a full analysis environment.

3. **Introduce simple forecasting for spend and revenue**  
   Add a basic forecasting model to help anticipate seasonal peaks and plan budgets more effectively, even with simple trend or smoothing models.

---

**Summary:**  
The VicBol dashboard provides a solid high-level view of performance and reveals clear opportunities to improve channel efficiency, budget allocation, and seasonal strategy.
