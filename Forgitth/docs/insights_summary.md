# Forggith Insights Summary
(Business Insights from Sales & Marketing Performance Dashboard)

This document summarizes the key insights derived from Forggith Pharmaceuticals’ Sales & Marketing Performance Dashboard.
Insights are based on the consolidated sales dataset (2022–2025), target data, and performance metrics.

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Forggith achieved $11.12bn in Actual Revenue, surpassing the $8.45bn Target Revenue by a significant margin.
Performance varied across months, teams, cities, product classes, and sales representatives, revealing clear strengths and improvement opportunities.

---

### 2. Revenue Trends & Time Intelligence

#### 2.1 Actual Revenue by Month

Revenue fluctuated across the year, with February showing the highest month‑to‑month increase.

January recorded the lowest MoM change, creating a strong upward trend into Q1.

MoM % change ranged from 0.00% to 6.0%, indicating moderate seasonality

### 9. Key Takeaways
Forggith is consistently outperforming targets across all years.

February shows the strongest month‑to‑month momentum.

Butzbach, Analgesics, and Ionclotide are major revenue drivers.

Charlie team is the standout performer.

Sales reps Abigail, Jimmy, and Sheila are top contributors.

Sub‑channels Private and Institution outperform Retail.

Revenue growth is strong but slightly slowed in 2025 YTD.
